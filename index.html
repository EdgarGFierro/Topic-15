Grammar & Time-Marker Cheat-Sheet

1. Present Perfect

have / has + Past Participle

Unfinished time, life experiences, or past actions connected to now.

Markers: already, yet, just, ever, never, since, for, twice, three times.

2. Simple Past

Verb-ed / Irregular V2

Completed actions at a closed, specific time in the past.

Markers: yesterday, last week/month, ... ago, when I was..., in 2018.

3. Present Continuous

am / is / are + Verb-ing

Actions happening right at this exact moment.

Markers: right now, at the moment, Look!, Be quiet!
